Fundamentals & Science

Understanding Quantum Technology

Quantum computing harnesses counter-intuitive phenomena of subatomic physics to process complex data exponentially faster than classical supercomputers.

⚡

Bits vs. Qubits

Classical computers use bits as the basic unit of information, strictly existing as a 0 or 1. Quantum computers use qubits, which leverage physical particles like photons or electrons.

🌀

Superposition

Superposition allows a qubit to exist in a linear combination of states (both $|0\rangle$ and $|1\rangle$) simultaneously. It enables quantum machines to evaluate millions of possibilities at once.

$$\psi = \alpha|0\rangle + \beta|1\rangle$$
🔗

Quantum Entanglement

When qubits become entangled, the quantum state of one instantly dictates the state of another—even across vast distances. Einstein famously called this "spooky action at a distance."

🎯

Quantum Interference

Quantum algorithms exploit wave-like interference to amplify correct paths to solutions while canceling out incorrect paths, dramatically narrowing down complex solutions.
